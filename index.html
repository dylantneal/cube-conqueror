    Solve any Rubik's cube

    Turn it yourself

    Drag any face of the cube, or use these buttons. On a keyboard, press U, D, L, R, F or B, and hold Shift to turn the other way.

    Enter your cube

    Paint the stickers

    1. Hold your cube with white on top and green facing you.
    2. Pick a color, then tap stickers to match your cube.
    3. Drag the background to see the back and bottom.

    The bright pieces on the cube are the ones this step fixes. Everything else is dimmed.
