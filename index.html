    Welcome

    Solve any Rubik's cube

    Paint your cube's colors onto the 3D cube, get the shortest solution, and learn what every move is doing.

    Turn it yourself

    Drag any face of the cube, or use these buttons. On a keyboard, press U, D, L, R, F or B, and hold Shift to turn the other way.

    How it works

    Enter your cube

    Hold your cube with white on top and green facing you. Pick a color, then tap the matching stickers on the 3D cube. Cube Conqueror checks that your colors make a real, solvable cube before it solves.

    Get the shortest solution

    A two-phase solver searches for the shortest solution it can find, usually around 20 moves, right in your browser.

    Learn every move

    Follow the solution one move at a time with Next and Back. An arrow shows each turn, the pieces each step fixes light up, and every step explains why it works. Stage one points every piece the right way. Stage two brings every piece home.

    Free and private

    No account, no download. Everything runs on your device.

    1. Hold your cube with white on top and green facing you.
    2. Pick a color, then tap stickers to match your cube.

    The bright pieces on the cube are the ones this step fixes. Everything else is dimmed.
